## Section 4 — Retention Bonus

4.1. **Retention Award.** In the event of a Change of Control of the
Company, Employee shall be entitled to a Retention Bonus in the gross
amount of one million two hundred fifty thousand dollars ($1,250,000),
payable as follows: fifty percent (50%) upon the closing of the Change
of Control, and fifty percent (50%) on the first anniversary of such
closing, subject to Employee's continued employment with the surviving
entity through such dates.

4.2. **Acceleration.** The unpaid portion of the Retention Bonus shall
accelerate and become immediately payable upon termination of Employee's
employment by the surviving entity without Cause or by Employee for
Good Reason.

4.3. **Cause; Good Reason.** "Cause" and "Good Reason" shall have the
meanings set forth in Schedule A. (Schedule A is not present in the
data room as of 2026-04-30.)

## Section 7 — Trigger Events

7.1. **Good Reason.** For purposes of this Agreement, "Good Reason"
includes, without limitation, (a) a material reduction in Employee's
base salary, (b) a material change in Employee's title or duties, (c)
a relocation of Employee's principal place of employment by more than
fifty (50) miles, or (d) any change in Employee's reporting line such
that Employee no longer reports directly to the Chief Executive Officer.

## Section 9 — Restrictive Covenants

9.1. **Non-Competition.** During Employee's employment with the Company
and for a period of twenty-four (24) months following the termination
of such employment for any reason, Employee shall not, directly or
indirectly, engage in, own, manage, operate, control, be employed by,
or participate in the ownership, management, operation, or control of
any business that competes with the Company anywhere in the United
States.

9.2. **Non-Solicitation.** For a period of eighteen (18) months following
the termination of Employee's employment for any reason, Employee shall
not solicit any customer, client, or employee of the Company.
