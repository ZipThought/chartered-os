## Article 7 — Indemnification

7.1. **General Indemnification.** Subject to the limitations set forth in
this Article 7, Seller shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Buyer
from and against any and all Losses arising out of or resulting from any
breach of any representation, warranty, covenant, or agreement of Seller
contained in this Agreement.

7.2. **Cap.** Seller's aggregate liability under Section 7.1 shall not
exceed a commercially reasonable amount, and in no event shall such
amount be subject to renegotiation following the Closing Date.

7.3. **Basket.** No indemnification claim shall be brought under
Section 7.1 unless and until the aggregate amount of all such Losses
exceeds one and one-half percent (1.5%) of the Purchase Price (the
"Basket"), at which point the Buyer may recover all Losses, including
those within the Basket, subject to the cap in Section 7.2.

7.4. **Survival.** The representations and warranties shall survive the
Closing Date for a period of eighteen (18) months, except for those set
forth in Sections 3.1 (Organization), 3.2 (Authority), and 3.5 (Title to
Assets), which shall survive indefinitely.

## Article 8 — Material Adverse Change

8.1. **Definition.** "Material Adverse Change" means any event,
circumstance, or condition that, individually or in the aggregate, has
had or would reasonably be expected to have a material adverse effect on
the business, operations, or financial condition of the Seller, taken as
a whole, **excluding** any change resulting from (a) general economic
conditions, (b) conditions affecting the industry in which Seller
operates generally, (c) changes in law or regulation, (d) acts of war,
terrorism, or pandemic, (e) climate-related events including but not
limited to wildfires, flooding, and storm damage, and (f) any failure
of Seller to meet projections, forecasts, or estimates of revenue,
earnings, or other financial measures.

8.2. **Closing Condition.** The absence of a Material Adverse Change
between the date hereof and the Closing Date is a condition to Buyer's
obligation to consummate the Closing.

## Article 9 — Working Capital Adjustment

9.1. **Reference Working Capital.** "Reference Working Capital" means the
working capital of Seller as of December 31, 2024, calculated in
accordance with Schedule 9.1.

9.2. **Adjustment.** The Purchase Price shall be adjusted dollar-for-dollar
by the difference between the Closing Date Working Capital and the
Reference Working Capital.
