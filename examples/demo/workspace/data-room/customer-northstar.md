annual renewal. **Annual contract value:** $14.7M (FY2025). Bates:

## Section 11 — Assignment and Change of Control

11.1. **Assignment.** Neither party may assign or transfer this Agreement,
in whole or in part, by operation of law or otherwise, without the prior
written consent of the other party, which consent shall not be
unreasonably withheld; provided, however, that NorthStar may withhold
consent in its sole discretion in the event of a Change of Control of
Vendor.

11.2. **Change of Control Defined.** A "Change of Control" of Vendor
shall be deemed to have occurred upon (a) the acquisition of more than
fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity of Vendor by any person or
group, (b) a sale of all or substantially all of Vendor's assets, or
(c) a merger or consolidation of Vendor in which Vendor is not the
surviving entity.

11.3. **Notice.** Vendor shall provide NorthStar with not less than
sixty (60) days' written notice of any anticipated Change of Control.
NorthStar shall have thirty (30) days from receipt of such notice to
elect, in its sole discretion, to (a) consent to the Change of Control,
(b) terminate this Agreement effective as of the Change of Control date,
or (c) require Vendor to negotiate amended terms reflecting the new
ownership.

## Section 14 — Term and Termination

14.1. **Initial Term.** Three (3) years from the Effective Date.

14.2. **Renewal.** This Agreement shall automatically renew for
successive one (1) year terms unless either party provides written
notice of non-renewal not less than ninety (90) days prior to the end
of the then-current term.

14.3. **Termination for Convenience.** NorthStar may terminate this
Agreement at any time and for any reason upon thirty (30) days' written
notice to Vendor, with a pro-rata refund of pre-paid fees. Vendor
shall have no equivalent right.
