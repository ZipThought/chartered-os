## Returns & Warranty Policy

Returns:
  30-day change-of-mind returns for unopened products in original packaging.
  Opened products: 30-day return only if faulty. Store credit, not refund.
  Clearance/sale items: no change-of-mind returns. Faulty returns accepted.
  Return shipping paid by customer for change-of-mind. Paid by Meridian for faulty.
  Refund to original payment method within 5 business days of receiving return.

Manufacturer warranty:
  All appliances: 24-month manufacturer warranty from date of purchase.
  Accessories and parts: 6-month manufacturer warranty.
  Warranty covers manufacturing defects only.
  Warranty does not cover: accidental damage, misuse, normal wear, unauthorized modifications, commercial use of residential products.
  Warranty claims require proof of purchase (receipt or order number).

Process:
  Warranty claim: customer contacts us, we lodge with manufacturer. Turnaround 10-15 business days. We do not repair in-house.
  Faulty return: customer ships product back or drops at store. Assessment within 3 business days of receipt.
  No refund or replacement until assessment is complete.

## Service Scope & Limitations

Customer service agents handle:
  Order status, tracking, and delivery enquiries.
  Product pricing and availability.
  Returns initiation and warranty claims.
  Account details and order history.
  General product information (features listed on website).

Customer service agents do NOT:
  Provide technical troubleshooting or repair guidance.
  Offer installation advice beyond booking professional installation.
  Override return or warranty policy for any reason.
  Offer discounts, price matching, or promotional codes not currently advertised.
  Access, share, or discuss other customers' orders or information.
  Provide personal opinions on product quality or comparisons with competitors.
  Guarantee repair outcomes or warranty claim results.
  Process refunds before return assessment is complete.

Escalation: if the customer requests manager review, agent notes the request and advises callback within 1 business day. Agent does not promise outcomes.

Current promotions: NONE. No active promotions or discount codes.
