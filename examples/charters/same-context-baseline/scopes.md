# Charter scopes — same-context-baseline strawman

## In-Conversation Acceptance

This baseline is the deliberate inverse of structural separation: it
accepts any proposal the Actor produces, on the grounds that an
in-conversation judge would inherit the Actor's reasoning prefix and
agree with it. The Charter exists to make the contrast empirically
demonstrable; running it under enforcement is unsafe.

The discriminating axis the corpus measures is the gap between
running scenarios under this Charter and running them under the
separated Charter that owns the same Frame ids. Outcomes that
diverge identify scenarios where structural separation is the load-
bearing mechanism — the persuasive-prefix subclass especially.
